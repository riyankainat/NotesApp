Thank you for using Notely. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Notely handles information when you use our offline notes application.

Notely is designed to help you create, edit, organize, save, and share your personal notes.

1. Information We Collect

Notely is designed to work as an offline notes application. The information you create or enter in the app may include:

  • Note titles and descriptions
  • Text formatting and text colors
  • Categories and folders
  • Images added to notes
  • Favorite and pinned note status
  • Note creation and update information

Notely does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. However, you may voluntarily include personal information in your notes.

2. How Information Is Used

Information created within Notely is used to provide the app's features, including:

  • Creating and saving notes
  • Editing existing notes
  • Organizing notes into categories
  • Adding and displaying images
  • Pinning important notes
  • Marking notes as favorites
  • Deleting notes
  • Saving text formatting and colors
  • Generating note images for sharing

3. Storage of Your Notes

Notely is designed to store notes locally on your device so that you can access them offline.

Important: Your notes are intended to remain on your device unless you choose to share them using an available sharing feature.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your notes or personal content to third parties.

If you uninstall Notely or clear the application's data, locally stored notes and related information may be permanently removed.

4. Image Sharing and WhatsApp

Notely allows you to share your notes as images using compatible applications installed on your device.

When you choose to share a note as an image:

  • The app generates an image of the note you select.
  • Your device may display a sharing menu with compatible applications such as WhatsApp.
  • You choose the application and recipient with whom you want to share the image.
  • The selected application handles the image after you choose to share it.

Notely does not independently send your notes to WhatsApp or other applications without your action.

Once you share an image through a third-party application, that application's privacy policy and terms of service apply to the shared content.

5. Images and Media Access

If you choose to add an image to a note, Notely may use the photo picker or relevant device permissions to allow you to select images.

Access to photos and media is used to support the app's image-related features.

You can manage or revoke application permissions through your device settings.

6. Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, Notely may request permissions related to:

  • Photos or media access, to allow you to select images for your notes.
  • File or storage access, where required by your device or Android version.

Notely only uses permissions needed to provide its features.

7. Third-Party Services

Notely is designed to function offline and does not intentionally transmit your personal notes to our servers.

When you use third-party applications to share content, those applications may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If analytics, advertising, crash reporting, cloud storage, or other external services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their data practices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting access to your notes.

9. Children's Privacy

Notely does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

10. Data Deletion

You can delete notes and other content using the deletion features provided within the application.

If information is stored locally on your device, deleting the content, clearing app data, or uninstalling Notely may remove that information.

Images shared through third-party applications may remain with the recipients or those applications. You may need to manage shared content separately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page. Please review this page periodically for changes.
