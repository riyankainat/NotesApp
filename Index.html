Thank you for using NotesApp. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how NotesApp handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

NotesApp is designed to help you create, edit, organize and manage your personal notes.

Depending on how you use the application, information entered or created within the app may include:

  • Note titles
  • Note descriptions
  • Text formatting and text colors
  • Categories
  • Images added to notes
  • Favorite and pinned note status
  • Note creation and update information

NotesApp does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily include such information in your notes.

2. How Information Is Used

Information created within NotesApp is used to provide and operate the features of the application, including:

  • Creating and saving notes
  • Editing existing notes
  • Organizing notes into categories
  • Adding and displaying images
  • Pinning important notes
  • Marking notes as favorites
  • Deleting and restoring notes
  • Saving text formatting and colors

3. Storage of Your Notes

Notes and related content may be stored locally on your device, depending on the application's configuration.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your notes or personal content to third parties.

If you uninstall NotesApp or clear the application's data, locally stored information may be removed according to your device's operating system behavior.

4. Images and Media

If you choose to add an image to a note, NotesApp may request access to photos, media, or files on your device.

This access is used to allow you to select and use images within your notes.

NotesApp does not intentionally access your photos or media unless you provide the required permission or select an image through the relevant system picker.

5. Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, NotesApp may request permissions related to:

  • Photos or media access, to allow you to select images for your notes.
  • File or storage access, where required by your device or Android version.

You can manage or revoke application permissions through your device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

NotesApp does not intentionally sell or share your personal note content with third parties.

If third-party services such as analytics, advertising, crash reporting, cloud services, or other external services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their data practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting access to your notes.

8. Children's Privacy

NotesApp does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete notes and other content using the deletion features provided within the application.

If information is stored locally on your device, deleting the content or uninstalling NotesApp may remove that locally stored information, depending on your device and operating system.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or NotesApp's privacy practices, please contact us.
